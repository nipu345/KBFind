---
title: Wired Ethernet Port Activation
tags: [network, hardware]
---
## Symptoms
- A computer plugged into a wall network jack shows "no internet" or an unidentified network.
- The network light on the computer or jack is off, or Wi-Fi works but the cable does not.

## Cause
Wall jacks in offices and labs are disabled until network operations activates them, so an inactive jack is the most common reason. A damaged cable or a computer whose network adapter is turned off can also cause it.

## Resolution
1. Try a different cable and, if possible, a different wall jack to rule out the cable.
2. Confirm the computer's wired network adapter is enabled.
3. Record the jack number printed on the wall plate (for example, 2B-114-03).
4. Submit a port activation request with the jack number, room, and the computer's wired MAC address.
5. Activations are usually completed within 2 business days. Do not move phones or printers between jacks, because some ports are set up for one device type.
