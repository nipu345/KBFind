---
title: Laptop Battery Health and Charging Faults
tags: [hardware]
---
## Symptoms
- The laptop says "plugged in, not charging," or the battery drains quickly even when new.
- The laptop shuts off suddenly at 20% or 30% charge.
- The battery looks swollen, or the case or trackpad is bulging.

## Cause
Batteries wear out over time. Battery health settings may also intentionally stop charging at 80% to extend battery life. Damaged chargers and loose charging ports are common causes as well.

## Resolution
1. If the battery or case is swollen, have the user stop using and charging the laptop immediately, and log an urgent hardware ticket. Swollen batteries can be a fire hazard.
2. Check battery health in system settings. A battery below 80% of original capacity is due for replacement.
3. Check whether a battery-saving charge limit is turned on before treating "not charging" as a fault.
4. Try a different charger and outlet, and look for debris in the charging port.
5. Update the operating system and firmware.
6. If the battery is worn out or charging still fails, log a hardware ticket for battery replacement.
