---
title: Lost or Stolen Device Reporting and Remote Wipe
tags: [security, mobile, hardware]
---
## Symptoms
- A user's university laptop, tablet, or phone has been lost or stolen.
- A personal phone that has university email or MFA on it is missing.

## Cause
A missing device may still be signed in to email and other university systems. Acting quickly limits the chance that someone else uses it to access accounts or data.

## Resolution
1. Have the user change their password right away from another device.
2. Remove the missing device from the user's MFA methods and sign out all active sessions in the account security page.
3. For university-owned devices, lock or wipe the device from the device management console and record the asset tag.
4. For personal phones, the user can erase the phone through the phone maker's find-my-device service.
5. Have the user file a police report for stolen devices, and report the loss to the security team if the device held sensitive data.
6. Set up the user's replacement device and re-enroll MFA.
