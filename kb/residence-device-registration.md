---
title: Residence Hall Device Registration for Gaming Consoles and Smart Devices
tags: [network, wireless]
---
## Symptoms
- A student's game console, smart TV, or streaming stick cannot connect to the residence hall Wi-Fi.
- The device does not offer a place to type a username and password.

## Cause
Consoles and smart devices cannot sign in to the secure campus network because it requires a personal username and password. They must be registered by hardware (MAC) address on the device network instead.

## Resolution
1. Find the device's wireless MAC address in its network settings menu.
2. If the device uses a private or randomized address, turn that off for the device network first.
3. Have the student register the address in the device registration portal while signed in with their own account.
4. Connect the device to the device network. Registration takes up to 15 minutes to activate.
5. Each student may register up to 8 devices. Remove old devices in the portal if the limit is reached.
