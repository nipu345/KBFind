---
title: Audio Device Selection in Video Conferencing Clients
tags: [software, audio]
---
## Symptoms
- Other people on a video call cannot hear the user, or the user cannot hear them.
- Microphone works in one app but not in the meeting client.
- Echo or feedback during calls.

## Cause
The meeting client is using a different microphone or speaker than expected (for example, a monitor's built-in speaker or a disconnected headset), or the operating system has blocked microphone access for the app.

## Resolution
1. In the meeting client's audio settings, select the correct microphone and speaker and use the test button.
2. Check the OS privacy settings and allow microphone access for the meeting app.
3. Confirm the user is not muted in the client and that any headset mute switch is off.
4. For echo, have participants who are in the same room mute all but one device.
5. Restart the client after plugging in a new headset so it detects the device.
