---
title: New Employee Account Provisioning
tags: [accounts, identity]
---
## Symptoms
- A new employee started today but cannot sign in to email or the employee portal.
- The new hire has an account but cannot reach department systems or shared drives.
- A manager asks how to get accounts set up before someone's first day.

## Cause
Accounts are created automatically once human resources finishes hiring paperwork in the HR system. If paperwork is incomplete or the start date is wrong, the account is not created. Department-specific access is requested separately by the manager.

## Resolution
1. Confirm the employee's hire is complete in the HR system and the start date is correct.
2. Accounts are created the business day after HR completes the hire. Have the employee activate their account from the claim-account page using their employee ID.
3. Set up MFA during account activation.
4. Have the manager request shared drives, mailing lists, and application access through the access request form.
5. If the hire is complete and the account still does not exist after one business day, escalate to the identity team with the employee ID.
