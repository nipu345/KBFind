---
title: Docking Station Peripherals Not Detected
tags: [hardware, desktop]
---
## Symptoms
- When the laptop is connected to the dock, the monitors, keyboard, mouse, or wired network do not work.
- The laptop does not charge through the dock.
- Everything worked before a recent update or laptop replacement.

## Cause
Docks need firmware and driver updates to work with newer laptops and operating systems. A dock with too little power for the laptop, a cable plugged into the wrong USB-C port, or a dock that has stopped responding can cause the same symptoms.

## Resolution
1. Unplug the dock's power cable for 30 seconds, plug it back in, and reconnect the laptop.
2. Make sure the dock cable is plugged into the laptop's Thunderbolt or charging USB-C port.
3. Install the dock driver and firmware updates from the software portal.
4. Confirm the dock's power adapter meets the laptop's power needs.
5. Test the peripherals plugged directly into the laptop to rule out a faulty monitor or cable.
6. If the dock still does not work, swap it with a known-good dock and log a hardware ticket.
