---
title: Lecture Capture Recording Failures
tags: [classroom, software, audio]
---
## Symptoms
- A scheduled class recording did not appear in the course after the lecture.
- The recording has video of the slides but no audio, or audio but no screen.
- An instructor wants to start recording a class that was not scheduled.

## Cause
Classroom recordings run on a schedule tied to the room. Recordings fail when the schedule has the wrong room or time, the room microphone is muted or not worn, or the instructor's laptop is not connected through the room input that is captured.

## Resolution
1. Check the recording schedule in the lecture capture portal and confirm the room and time match the class.
2. Allow up to 2 hours after the class ends for processing before treating the recording as missing.
3. Confirm the instructor wears the room's lapel microphone and that the mute light is off.
4. Have the instructor connect laptops to the room's main input, since other inputs may not be recorded.
5. For unscheduled recordings, use the record button on the room control panel.
6. If a scheduled recording is missing, submit a ticket with the room, date, and time so the room recorder can be checked.
