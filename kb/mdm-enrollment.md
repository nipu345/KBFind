---
title: Mobile Device Management Enrollment for University Apps
tags: [mobile, security]
---
## Symptoms
- A user's phone shows a message that it must be enrolled or "managed" before university email or apps will work.
- The user is worried enrolling will let IT see personal photos or messages.

## Cause
Some university apps and data require basic device protections, such as a screen lock and an up-to-date operating system. App protection enrollment checks those settings. It does not give IT access to personal photos, messages, or browsing.

## Resolution
1. Explain that enrollment only manages university apps and data, and that personal content is not visible.
2. Update the phone to a supported operating system version.
3. Set a screen lock PIN or passcode if the phone has none.
4. Install the company portal app from the app store and sign in with the university account.
5. Follow the prompts to register the device, then reopen the university app.
6. If enrollment fails, have the user remove and reinstall the company portal app and try again on Wi-Fi.
