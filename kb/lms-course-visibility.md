---
title: Learning Management System Course Visibility and Enrollment
tags: [software, classroom, accounts]
---
## Symptoms
- A student cannot see a course they are registered for in the learning management system.
- An instructor cannot see a course they are teaching, or students cannot see the course content.
- A student who added a class late does not appear in the roster.

## Cause
Courses are created and enrollments are updated from the registration system on a regular sync, usually several times a day. Courses also stay hidden from students until the instructor publishes them.

## Resolution
1. Confirm the student is registered in the registration system, not just on a waitlist.
2. Allow up to 24 hours after a registration change for the enrollment to sync.
3. Ask the instructor whether the course has been published. Unpublished courses do not appear for students.
4. For instructors, confirm they are listed as instructor of record in the registration system.
5. For teaching assistants or guest instructors, the instructor can add them manually in course settings.
6. If enrollment is still missing after 24 hours, submit a ticket with the student's ID and the course section number.
