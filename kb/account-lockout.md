---
title: Account Lockout Remediation via Identity Management Portal
tags: [accounts, identity]
---
## Symptoms
- User cannot sign in to email, the student portal, or campus Wi-Fi.
- Error says the account is locked, or keeps saying the password is incorrect even though it is typed correctly.

## Cause
Too many failed sign-in attempts in a short window. The most common source is an old password still saved on a phone, tablet, or laptop that keeps retrying in the background after a password change.

## Resolution
1. Verify the user's identity before making any account changes.
2. In the identity management portal, confirm the lockout and note the time of the last failed attempts.
3. Unlock the account. Lockouts also clear on their own after 30 minutes.
4. Have the user update or remove the saved password on every device (phone mail app, Wi-Fi profile, laptop keychain).
5. If the account locks again within an hour, check the failed-attempt log for the device name and escalate if it is unfamiliar.
