---
title: Desk Phone Voicemail PIN Reset and Call Forwarding
tags: [hardware, accounts]
---
## Symptoms
- A user forgot their voicemail PIN or is locked out after too many attempts.
- The desk phone shows "no service" or "registering."
- The user wants calls forwarded to their mobile phone while working remotely.

## Cause
Voicemail locks after several wrong PIN entries. Desk phones lose service when the network cable is disconnected or the phone is plugged into a jack that is not set up for phones.

## Resolution
1. Reset the voicemail PIN in the phone self-service portal, or verify the user's identity and reset it in the phone administration console.
2. Have the user dial into voicemail and set a new PIN.
3. For a phone showing "no service," check that the cable goes from the wall jack to the phone's network port, not the PC port.
4. Restart the phone by unplugging it for 10 seconds.
5. To forward calls, the user can set call forwarding in the self-service portal or use the forward key on the phone.
6. Voicemail messages can also be sent to email as audio attachments if the user prefers.
