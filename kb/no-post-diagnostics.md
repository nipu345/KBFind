---
title: Hardware No-POST Diagnostics
tags: [hardware, desktop]
---
## Symptoms
- Laptop will not turn on, or turns on with lights and fan but the screen stays black.
- Computer beeps or flashes a light pattern at startup.

## Cause
Power delivery failure, a fully drained battery, a failed memory module, or a display problem. The device is not completing its power-on self test (POST).

## Resolution
1. Connect the original charger and wait 15 minutes before trying again; confirm the charging light comes on.
2. Hold the power button for 30 seconds to drain residual power, then press it normally.
3. Connect an external monitor to tell a dead display apart from a system that is not booting.
4. Record any beep code or light pattern and look it up in the manufacturer's diagnostic guide.
5. If there is still no POST, check warranty status and open a hardware repair ticket. Back up data first if the drive is accessible.
