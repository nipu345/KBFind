---
title: Departing Employee Offboarding and Data Handoff
tags: [accounts, identity, storage]
---
## Symptoms
- An employee is leaving and the manager needs access to their email, files, or shared mailbox ownership.
- A departed employee's account is still active or they are still on mailing lists.
- A former employee asks why they lost access to email.

## Cause
Account access ends automatically on the separation date entered in the HR system. Files and mailboxes owned only by the departing employee may become hard to reach afterward unless ownership is moved before they leave.

## Resolution
1. Before the last day, have the employee transfer ownership of shared files and folders to a colleague.
2. Have the employee hand off ownership of any mailing lists, shared mailboxes, and shared calendars.
3. The manager can request access to the departed employee's mailbox or files for up to 30 days after separation, with HR approval.
4. University-owned devices must be returned on or before the last day so they can be wiped and reassigned.
5. If the account was disabled too early or too late, confirm the separation date with HR, which controls account timing.
