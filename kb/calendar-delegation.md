---
title: Calendar Sharing and Delegate Permissions
tags: [email, calendar]
---
## Symptoms
- An assistant cannot see or book meetings on their manager's calendar.
- Meeting invitations go to the wrong person, or both the manager and delegate get every invite.
- A shared calendar shows only free/busy blocks instead of meeting details.

## Cause
Calendar access is controlled by the calendar owner. Viewing details, editing, and acting as a delegate are separate permission levels, and delegate settings also control where meeting invites are delivered.

## Resolution
1. Have the calendar owner open calendar sharing settings and add the assistant.
2. Choose the right level: "can view all details" to see meetings, "can edit" to make changes, or "delegate" to accept invites on the owner's behalf.
3. For delegates, choose whether invites go to the delegate only or to both people.
4. Have the assistant remove and re-add the shared calendar if it still shows only free/busy.
5. Permissions can take up to an hour to sync to desktop and mobile clients.
