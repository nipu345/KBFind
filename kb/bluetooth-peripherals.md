---
title: Bluetooth Keyboard, Mouse, and Headset Pairing Problems
tags: [hardware, audio]
---
## Symptoms
- A wireless keyboard, mouse, or headset will not connect, or keeps disconnecting.
- The cursor stutters or typed letters appear late.
- A headset connects but audio is poor quality when the microphone is in use.

## Cause
Low batteries, pairing records saved on more than one computer, distance or interference from other wireless devices, and outdated Bluetooth drivers are the usual causes. Headsets switch to a lower-quality mode whenever the microphone is active.

## Resolution
1. Charge or replace the device batteries.
2. Remove the device from the computer's Bluetooth list, put the device in pairing mode, and pair it again.
3. If the device came with a USB receiver, plug the receiver into a port on the computer rather than into a hub or monitor.
4. Move the computer away from other wireless devices and metal obstructions.
5. Update the computer's operating system to get current Bluetooth drivers.
6. For headset audio quality, use a separate microphone or a wired headset for calls.
