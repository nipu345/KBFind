---
title: Mailing List Membership and Distribution Group Management
tags: [email]
---
## Symptoms
- A user is not receiving messages sent to their department or class mailing list.
- Messages sent to a list bounce back with "you do not have permission to send to this group."
- A list owner has left and nobody can add or remove members.

## Cause
Distribution lists are managed by named owners, and many lists only accept messages from members or approved senders. Lists without an active owner cannot be changed by regular users.

## Resolution
1. Look up the list in the group management portal to see its owners and members.
2. Ask a list owner to add the user, or to add them as an approved sender if they only need to send.
3. If the user is a member but still not receiving messages, check their junk folder and any inbox rules that move list messages.
4. If every owner has left, a department head can request ownership be reassigned through a service request.
5. Messages to large lists may be held for moderation; the sender will receive a notice when this happens.
